About Campnio

Dating apps optimized for swipes. We optimized for endings.

Campnio was built by people who were exhausted by the same three apps everyone else was using — endless matches, no follow-through, and conversations that went nowhere. So we built something slower, smaller, and considerably more serious about the outcome.

  • Verified people

    Every profile on Campnio is reviewed by a real person before it ever goes live — not a bot, not a checkbox.

  • Fewer, better matches

    We surface a small number of considered introductions each week instead of an infinite scroll of options.

  • Built for intent

    Campnio is for people who already know they want a real relationship — not another maybe.

Features

Everything about Campnio is built around one question: is this person right for you?

Compatibility Intelligence

Our matching model weighs values, life pace, and long-term intentions far more heavily than looks or bios — the things that actually predict whether two people last.

Human-Verified Profiles

Real ID checks and manual review before any profile is visible to the community.

Curated Weekly Introductions

No endless feed. A short, considered list of people worth your time, every week.

Guided First Conversations

Prompts drawn from your shared answers, so the first message is never "hey."

Private by Default

You control exactly who sees your profile, and your data is never sold — ever.
